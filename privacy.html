Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Noticias is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and publishes news bulletins to the owner's own accounts. This policy explains what data it handles.

1. Whose data

The only account data Noticias handles is its owner's. It has no users besides the owner, and it does not collect, request or store data about anyone else, including people who watch the bulletins or visit this website.

2. What is stored, and where

When the owner connects TikTok, Noticias receives from TikTok an access token, a refresh token, the scopes granted and the account's open ID. It also reads the account's display name, only to show which account is connected. YouTube works the same way with its own tokens.

These tokens are kept in a file on the owner's computer. They are not sent anywhere except back to TikTok or YouTube to make the uploads the owner asked for.

3. How it is used

The tokens are used for one thing: uploading the owner's own bulletins to the owner's own accounts, and checking the status of those uploads. Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or analysed.

4. How long it is kept

Until the owner disconnects the account. Disconnecting from Noticias deletes the token file. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or from the Google account settings.

5. This website

This site is a set of static pages served by GitLab Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. GitLab, as the host, may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the operator's profile at gitlab.com/supertren.
