Noticias

Short daily news bulletins, with every headline linked to its source.

Noticias produces short vertical news videos in Spanish and English: world and national news, Ceuta, sport, science, economy, curiosities and mystery. Each bulletin picks the day's most relevant stories from public RSS news feeds and presents them with licensed stock footage, a voice-over and on-screen headlines.

Every bulletin ends with a QR code that opens its page on this site, where each headline links to the article it came from. All of those pages are listed below.

How it works

  • 1. ReadHeadlines are collected from public RSS feeds of established news outlets.
  • 2. SelectThe most relevant stories of the day are ranked and chosen, avoiding repeats.
  • 3. ProduceA vertical video is assembled with credited stock footage and a voice-over.
  • 4. PublishThe bulletin goes to the owner's YouTube and TikTok accounts, and its source page to this site.

Publishing to TikTok

Noticias uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. The owner connects their own TikTok account once; each bulletin is then uploaded to that account, either as a draft to finish in the TikTok app or, when the owner chooses to, as a direct post with the privacy the owner picks. Noticias is not a public service: there are no user sign-ups, and the only TikTok account it acts on is its owner's. See the Privacy Policy for the data involved.
